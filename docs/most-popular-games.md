# Most Popular Video Games

A quick list of some of the most popular games played across PC and consoles.

- **Minecraft** — Sandbox / Survival
  Best-selling game of all time with creative and survival gameplay modes.
- **Grand Theft Auto V** — Action / Open World
  Massive open-world crime sandbox with long-running online popularity.
- **Fortnite** — Battle Royale / Shooter
  Free-to-play multiplayer hit known for live events and cross-platform play.
- **League of Legends** — MOBA
  One of the most played competitive PC games worldwide.
- **Roblox** — Platform / User-Generated Games
  Huge player base with thousands of user-created game experiences.
- **Call of Duty: Warzone** — Battle Royale / FPS
  Fast-paced large-scale multiplayer shooter with regular seasonal updates.
