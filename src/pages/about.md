# About Us

Welcome to **All Things Video Games** — your one-stop encyclopedia for exploring the world of video games.

We created this site to be a simple, organized, and fun place where gamers of all levels can discover new titles, dive into different genres, and read insightful reviews.

---

## What You Can Find Here

- Genre-based game pages: Explore games organized by their genre.
- Game lists and highlights: Discover popular and hidden gem titles.
- Reviews and recommendations: Get opinions, ratings, and suggestions from fellow gamers.

---

## Our Goal

We want to make it easier for players to find games they enjoy and learn more about the games they love — whether you’re a casual player or a hardcore fan.

We hope you enjoy browsing, discovering, and sharing your favorite games with the community!
